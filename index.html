Leo Yu

I study how machines sound and vibrate. I use microphone arrays to locate noise sources and sparse signal models to uncover early signs of faults in bearings and gears.

Research directions

Acoustic imaging

I use microphone arrays and compressive beamforming to locate sound sources, including sources that fall between the points of a reconstruction grid.

Vibration signal processing

I develop sparse representations that separate repetitive impacts from background vibration and track fault signatures as shaft speed changes.

Machinery diagnostics

I study how the timing of impacts and the harmonic structure of gear vibration can reveal bearing and gearbox faults.
