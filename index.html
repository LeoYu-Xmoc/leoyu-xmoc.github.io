我研究机器的声音与振动：用传声器阵列寻找噪声源，从齿轮与轴承的微弱冲击中识别故障。I study how machines sound and vibrate. My work uses microphone arrays to locate noise sources and sparse signal models to identify faults in bearings and gears.

研究方向Research

声源识别Acoustic imaging

从传声器阵列采集的声压反推噪声源的位置。动态网格让声源位置随数据调整，CLEAN-SC 改进方法则关注主瓣重叠时的分辨能力。I use microphone arrays to locate noise sources. Dynamic grids refine source locations between grid points, while modifications to CLEAN-SC help distinguish sources whose main lobes overlap.

稀疏信号处理Sparse signal models

从背景振动中提取微弱冲击。通过自适应字典拟合冲击波形，用周期约束保留重复出现的故障特征，并用时频表示跟踪转速变化。Adaptive dictionaries fit impact waveforms, periodicity constraints recover repeating transients, and time–frequency models track fault signatures as shaft speed changes.

旋转机械诊断Rotating machinery

研究齿轮局部损伤怎样调制振动，以及怎样从风机实测信号估计轴速、分离行星齿轮和轴承的故障成分。I study how local gear damage modulates vibration and how shaft-speed estimation and synchronous averaging help isolate fault signatures in wind turbine drivetrains.

声束形成：把同一个波峰对齐Beamforming: lining up a wavefront

1 kHz 平面波从 +30° 入射，同一波峰依次到达各个传声器。改变扫描方向，观察补偿后的各通道波形及其平均值；拖到 +30° 时，峰值重合，输出最强。A 1 kHz plane wave arrives from +30°. Steer the array to compensate for the delay at each microphone. At +30°, the corrected peaks line up and their average reaches full amplitude.

故障诊断：从振动中找出重复冲击Fault diagnosis: finding repeated impacts

设轴频为 20 Hz，模拟缺陷每转产生三次冲击，激起 900 Hz 共振。比较健康与缺陷状态；包络谱中 60 Hz 及其倍频反映冲击的重复频率。The shaft turns at 20 Hz. A simulated defect strikes three times per revolution, exciting a 900 Hz resonance. Compare healthy and faulty signals: 60 Hz and its harmonics in the envelope spectrum reveal the impact repetition rate.
